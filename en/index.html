Our place in the village

More than a café: where Bera meets

You step in from the mountain chill and you're met by the smell of freshly brewed coffee and the day's pastries. At Sara Kafetegia we care for every cup as if it were for family, with properly textured milk, croissants fresh from the oven and tortilla pintxos for those who arrive hungry.

We're right in the centre of Bera, in the Bidasoa valley, a stone's throw from Lesaka, Etxalar and the French border. Inside there's a forest mural, shelves of books and plants; outside, a generous terrace overlooking the river.

The terrace beside the Bidasoa

When the sun comes out in Bera, there's one place where coffee tastes different: our terrace facing the river. Parasols, tables, decorative barrels and the sound of the Bidasoa in the background. Time doesn't rush here.

Perfect for breakfast in the open air, a glass of rosé at aperitif time, or simply gazing at the trees with a cup in your hand.

How we make our coffee

Unhurried, in three steps

Good coffee has no secrets: it has care. This is how we make it every day.

We choose good beans

Bera · Bortziriak

In the heart of the Bidasoa valley

Bera is one of the Bortziriak (Five Towns), set between the River Bidasoa and the mountains, with La Rhune (Larrun) watching over it from 905 metres and the French border a step away. A village of stone houses, of the romantic organ of San Esteban and of Itzea, the Baroja family home.

We're here for the people of Bera and for everyone passing through: hikers coming down from La Rhune, travellers on their way to France and neighbours from Lesaka, Etxalar or Igantzi who drop in for their coffee. And for anyone after a quiet morning on the terrace by the river.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most

Where is Sara Kafetegia?

You'll find us at Iturlandeta Karrika, 6, in the centre of Bera (Bera de Bidasoa), Navarre, in the Bortziriak district. We're a few minutes from Lesaka, Etxalar and Igantzi, and very close to the French border. We have an outdoor terrace beside the River Bidasoa.

What are your opening hours?

We're open Monday to Friday from 7:30 to 13:30 and from 17:00 to 20:30, and at weekends from 8:00 to 14:00 and from 17:00 to 20:30. Breakfast is served all morning.

What can I have?

Freshly brewed coffee (espresso, cortado, café con leche, cappuccino with latte art), breakfasts, a variety of pastries (croissants, chocolate napolitanas, doughnuts, palmiers), tortilla pintxos, filled rolls and wine for an aperitif. Also teas, herbal infusions, soft drinks and juices, with vegan options.

Do you have a terrace?

Yes, and it's one of the things we're known for. We have a spacious terrace with a portico, parasols, white tables, flowers and decorative barrels, looking straight onto the River Bidasoa and the trees along its banks. Perfect when the sun comes out.

Do you have pastries and pintxos?

Yes. Every day we have croissants, chocolate napolitanas, lemon doughnuts, palmiers and cream buns to go with your coffee. Our counter display also holds tortilla pintxos, a variety of filled rolls and French omelette. Ask at the counter for today's selection.

Can I order to take away?

Yes, we serve coffee, filled rolls and pastries to take away throughout our opening hours. It's a favourite with people passing through towards France or heading up La Rhune (Larrun).

We'll be waiting for you in Bera

On the terrace beside the Bidasoa or in the room with the forest mural. The first cup always tastes better unhurried.
