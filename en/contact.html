Getting here is easy

How to reach us

Bera is very well connected, between San Sebastián, Pamplona and the French border. Here are a few pointers.

By car

From Pamplona or San Sebastián, take the N-121-A following the Bidasoa. From France, cross at Ibardin or Lizuniaga. Once in the village, we're in the centre, right by the square.

Parking

There's parking on nearby streets and in the village's designated areas. Leave the car close by and walk over: the centre of Bera can be crossed in a moment.

On foot through the village

From the square, a minute's walk. From the car park, walk through the historic centre among the stone houses.
