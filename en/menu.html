About the menu

Frequently asked questions

What's in the display case each day?

The display case changes every day depending on what's freshly made: croissants, chocolate napolitanas, glazed doughnuts, palmiers, cream buns, tortilla rolls, ham, cheese… The best thing is to drop by and see what's there. If you have a preference, call us ahead of time.

Can I get coffee to take away?

Yes. We prepare any coffee or breakfast from the menu to take away, throughout our opening hours. It's very common among people heading up La Rhune (Larrun) or on their way to the French border.

Do you take orders for groups or afternoon get-togethers?

Yes. For groups, birthdays or afternoon get-togethers we can prepare trays of pastries and coffee. Call us on 948 62 56 73 a day ahead and we'll organise it.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, card (contactless too) and Bizum. For large group orders, we can prepare a single bill.

Do you have plant-based milk, vegan or gluten-free options?

Yes. We have oat, soy and almond milk for any coffee, at no extra cost, and every day some vegan option and, almost always, something gluten-free. Ask at the counter about today's options.

Feeling hungry?

Come by Sara Kafetegia and order at the counter. We'll prepare it fresh, to eat in or take away.
